Greenfield Institute of Technology - Student Handbook 2026
Library
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays. On Saturdays it is open from 9:00
AM to 5:00 PM. On Sundays the library is open only during exam weeks, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days. The late fine is Rs. 5 per book per day.
Attendance
Students must have at least 80% attendance in every subject to write the semester exam. Students
with 70% to 79% attendance may apply for condonation with a medical certificate. The condonation
fee is Rs. 500 per subject. Students below 70% must repeat the subject.
Hostel
Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM. Students returning late must inform the warden in advance. Hostel
complaints (water, electricity, room repairs) should be raised on the campus app under "Hostel
Helpdesk" or emailed to the chief warden, Mr. Ravi Menon. Mess timings are 7:00-9:00 AM, 12:30-2:00
PM and 7:30-9:30 PM.
Examinations
Internal exams are held in the 7th and 13th week of each semester. The final semester exam carries
60 marks and the internal assessment carries 40 marks. The pass mark is 40 out of 100, with at least
24 out of 60 in the final exam. Revaluation requests must be submitted within 7 days of results, with a
fee of Rs. 300 per paper.
Placements
The placement cell is on the second floor of the Admin Block. Students need a CGPA of 6.5 or above,
with no active backlogs, to register for campus placements. Placement training classes run every
Saturday from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM in the 6th and 7th semesters.
