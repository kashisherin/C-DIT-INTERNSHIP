Late registration requires approval from the academic coordinator and payment of a late registration fee of Rs. 250. Students must verify their registered subjects before the semester begins. Any changes to course registration must be completed within the first two weeks of the semester.

Students may withdraw from an elective course within the first four weeks of the semester with approval from the faculty advisor. Course withdrawal after the deadline is not permitted except in exceptional circumstances approved by the academic dean. A withdrawn course will appear as a withdrawal on the academic record and will not contribute to the semester GPA.

Students whose semester GPA falls below 5.0 will be placed on academic probation. Students on probation must meet their faculty advisor at least twice during the semester and prepare an academic improvement plan. Students who remain on probation for two consecutive semesters may be required to attend additional academic counseling.

Students who receive a grade below the minimum passing grade may register for a grade improvement examination in the next available semester. The examination fee is Rs. 400 per subject. The improved grade will replace the previous grade only if it is higher. Students must complete grade improvement registration before the announced deadline
