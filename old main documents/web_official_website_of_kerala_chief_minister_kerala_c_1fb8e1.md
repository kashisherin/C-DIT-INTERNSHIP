## Know the CHIEF MINISTER

V. D. Satheesan is the 13th Chief Minister of Kerala. Born on 31 May 1964 at Nettoor in Ernakulam district, he has been representing the Paravur Assembly Constituency since 2001.
He was first elected to the Kerala Legislative Assembly from the Paravur constituency in the 2001 Kerala Legislative Assembly election. He was subsequently re-elected in the Assembly elections held in 2006, 2011, 2016, 2021, and 2026.
